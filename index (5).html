📝 Blog — Levelling Up to SOC L2

POSTED BY BHAVESHXDR · TRYHACKME PATH LOG

Earning the SOC Level-Up badge marked the point where my TryHackMe grind shifted from scattered CTF rooms into a structured path: thinking like a SOC L2 analyst instead of just solving rooms for the sake of it. It's currently sitting at 0% epic rarity on my profile, which tells me most learners either haven't reached this stage yet or haven't stuck with the path long enough to unlock it.

Getting here wasn't about one big win — it was the accumulation of smaller ones. Streak badges (3-day, 7-day) kept me consistent. League placements (Bronze, Silver — both 1st place) pushed me to compete on time and accuracy, not just correctness. And the 3 Million Legend badge came from clearing a room in TryHackMe's special anniversary module, which was a good excuse to explore a corner of the platform I hadn't touched yet.

What actually changed in how I work, moving from L1-style thinking into L2:

  • Triage before action. Instead of reacting to every alert individually, I started grouping them by asset and looking for patterns — is this one failed login, or is it part of a spray?
  • Context over signatures. An IOC alone doesn't tell the full story. I now cross-reference against baseline behavior for that host or user before deciding if something is truly anomalous.
  • Documentation as a skill, not a chore. Every room I clear now gets a short writeup — what the alert was, what I checked, what I concluded, and why. That habit alone made my SOC-style reasoning noticeably sharper.
  • Escalation judgment. Knowing when something is a false positive versus when it needs to go up the chain is its own skill — the SOC L2 path pushes you to practice that call, not just detect the issue.

Next on the path: pushing further into the SOC Level 2 analyst track, layering in more detection-engineering practice, and feeding what I learn back into my home-lab XDR dashboard project so the theory turns into something I actually built and can point to.
